04 / RESEARCH EXPLAINED

Powerful AI. Simple Answers.

No complicated mathematics — just understand what the system does.

01 🎥

We look at your video

The system reads frames from your recording and looks at changes from moment to moment.

02 🐟

YOLO finds the fish

Your trained model draws boxes around visible goldfish. The confidence slider controls which detections are shown.

03 ⚡

We measure movement

Optical flow measures how much the image changes. This is an activity estimate, not a calibrated swimming speed.

04 📊

We explain the behaviour

Current movement is compared with your research baseline. Unusual movement alone does not prove hunger or illness.

Scientific transparency

Fish count represents visible detections in sampled frames, not guaranteed unique animals. Movement results can be influenced by lighting, reflections or camera changes. Actual feeding motivation needs validated behavioural observations, and fish health needs appropriate care assessment.
